AI disclosure

Issen is developed by Digital Ethos Global Gaming with AI assistance.

Development

AI coding tools have helped write and revise game code, design features and interfaces, investigate bugs, create tests and maintain documentation. The developer directs the work and reviews the game's behaviour and presentation.

Artwork

AI image generation has been used for character and equipment artwork, environment sprites, menu illustrations and sprite atlases. Generated artwork is selected and integrated into Issen's ink-and-paper style. Layout, tinting, animation and visual effects are also created through code.

While playing

The game uses built-in rules and bundled assets. It does not send gameplay or player saves to an AI service, and it does not generate AI responses or artwork during a run.
