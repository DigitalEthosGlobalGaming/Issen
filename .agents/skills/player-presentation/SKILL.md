---
name: player-presentation
description: Design or revise Issen player-facing menus, unlocks and feature explanations using concise copy and established visual cues.
---

Read the affected screen and nearby flows before adding copy. Keep Issen's sparse,
ink-and-paper voice and follow `docs/features/symbol-art.md` for symbols.

Prefer a clear action, visual state and short label. If colour, selection, a stripe
or an emblem already communicates a state, do not repeat it in a badge or paragraph.
Keep accessible names and semantic states even when the visible cue is purely visual.

Explain only what the player needs to choose or act. Do not list irrelevant systems
that a mode disables, implementation details, or all exceptions on its entry screen.
Reveal necessary detail at the point of use. Temple affordability needs its visual
state; Trials need their objective and reward, without a list of unrelated bonuses.

Use hierarchy and space before adding headings or descriptions. Review the actual
mobile flow for duplicated labels, competing emphasis and avoidable explanation.
