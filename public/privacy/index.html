Privacy Policy

Issen · Digital Ethos Global Gaming
Effective date: 30 September 2026

Issen saves your game progress on your device. The current game has no player accounts, advertising, gameplay analytics, or cloud save service.

What this policy covers

This policy covers the current Issen game, including its GitHub Pages website and Android app. Third-party hosting, fonts, support, and services you choose when sharing have their own privacy policies.

Game data stored on your device

Issen uses local storage to remember settings, scores, gameplay statistics, unlocked equipment, progression, tutorial status, and a checkpoint for continuing a run. These records use keys beginning with issen.. Testing tools use a separate local profile and a session flag.

The game does not upload these saves to us. They remain in your browser or the app's local storage until you reset your profile, clear browser or app data, or your device removes them. Browser saves belong to the website's origin and do not automatically transfer between browsers, devices, or the Android app.

To delete saved progress, use Stats → Reset profile → Delete all progress. You can also clear the site's storage in browser settings or clear the Android app's data in device settings. Deleting local data removes your saved progress.

Information the game does not collect

The current game does not request your name, email address, location, contacts, camera, or microphone. It does not send gameplay analytics to us, use advertising identifiers, sell personal information, or include advertising. The Android app can offer an optional real-money cosmetic purchase. The game does not set tracking cookies; local storage supports game functionality.

Optional Android purchases

When purchases are enabled in the Android app, Google Play handles payment and RevenueCat validates transactions and remembers Premium ownership. RevenueCat uses an anonymous app user identifier, purchase history and technical app/device information to provide this service. Network requests also expose connection information such as your IP address. We do not receive your payment card details or upload your gameplay saves to RevenueCat. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases are optional; the free game works offline. The browser version does not enable billing. Resetting gameplay progress does not cancel a purchase. You can restore Premium with the same Google Play account after reinstalling. For purchase-data privacy requests, contact us through the support link below; do not post receipts or identifiers publicly.

Website hosting and fonts

The web game and this policy are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages documentation.

The web game loads its typeface through Google Fonts. Font requests send Google your IP address and request information such as browser headers. See Google Fonts privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy. The Android prototype bundles its font locally. This policy page uses system fonts and does not request Google Fonts.

Sharing a score

If you choose to save or share a score card, Issen creates an image on your device and uses an available host download service, your device's share sheet, or a browser download. A service or app you choose may receive that image under its own privacy policy. Issen does not automatically post scores or upload the image to a game server.

Support and privacy questions

Contact Digital Ethos Global Gaming through the Issen GitHub support page. Information you submit there is handled through GitHub and may be publicly visible. Please do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. We use information you voluntarily provide to respond to your request.

Children's privacy

The current game does not ask players for personal information or knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support request, contact us through the support page so we can address it.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date when the game's data practices change, including if online services, advertising, or purchases are introduced.
