Changelog

Issen · What changed

1.29.1

  • The Crow companion now perches on the shoulder through lean and reaction poses.
  • Removed floating grass tufts attached to enemies; the animated field grass stays unchanged.

1.29.0

  • Throwing knives refill for every duel; Temple descriptions now explain the cadence.
  • Secrets survive restoring a saved encounter. Visit cinematic mode to unlock the floating Mystic Rock companion.
  • Awakening effects follow the shape and bends of each blade profile.

1.28.0

  • Ink is now the only artwork style; animated grass, weather, spears and awakening effects remain.
  • Artwork readiness is checked before the scene begins.

1.27.0

  • Added release notes from the title screen. The link highlights updates since your last visit until you open it.

1.26.1

  • Added ink artwork for the First Strike and Pilgrim’s Bead charms.

1.26.0

  • Artwork loads before the game starts, with progress and a retry option.

1.25.0

  • Charms now share modular ink artwork.

1.24.0

  • All player blades use modular ink blade, handle and guard artwork.

1.23.0

  • Foreground bamboo frames Bamboo Road on computers and tablets.

1.22.0

  • Ink companions and more varied enemy appearances.

1.21.1

  • Ink is now the default artwork style.

1.21.0

  • Modular ink outfits for the player.

1.20.0

  • Modular ink enemies join the scene.
