Changelog

Issen · What changed

1.50.3

  • Maintenance update to keep development checks and test captures organized.

1.50.2

  • Perfect cuts give clothing and charms a stronger, more satisfying sway.

1.50.1

  • Completed Trials carry a bold completion stroke over their artwork and card.
  • Small-screen scroll menus fit without horizontal overflow.
  • Imported saves preserve unfamiliar progress arrays for future versions.

1.50.0

  • Cuts and parries now give clothing and charms a small, natural sway, using the existing sprites.

1.49.0

  • Tap the Armoury player to inspect your equipment full screen.
  • The Armoury now fits each screen with a larger mobile player, responsive room composition and subtle movement by the window.

1.48.0

  • The game and all sound now freeze when the tab, window or app leaves view, resuming without lost time.
  • Mobile rendering does less repeated work in menus while keeping the same artwork and resolution.

1.47.1

  • Awakening forms now use distinct brush-edged plates, with an ivory rim for Normal and gold rims for higher forms.

1.47.0

  • Scroll menus are now the only menu style, with clearer end-of-run captions.
  • Armoury Details stays open while browsing; categories use scrolling without arrow buttons.
  • Awakening forms have textured silk controls and a clear gold selection state.

1.46.1

  • Completion brush strokes now span the whole Trial card behind its content.

1.46.0

  • Completed Trial buttons carry a brush-stroke mark while remaining replayable.
  • Demon Mirror scenery stays consistent across all thirteen waves.

1.45.0

  • Create and switch named profiles in Options → Profile Management; download or import a save on another device or browser.
  • Imports preserve earned progress, recover older saves and keep a previous backup.

1.44.0

  • Test upcoming changes in separate web previews while the main game remains available.

1.43.0

  • Choose Normal, Awakened or Third forms directly in the Armoury.
  • A smaller mobile preview and expandable details give your collection more room.
  • Browse categories with arrows and scan cleaner equipment tiles and effect descriptions.

1.42.0

  • Unlock Tanto in the Temple: one automatic defensive strike per rank, per run.
  • A more compact Armoury gives equipment more room.
  • Scattered Armour pieces now fly in varied directions.

1.41.0

  • Daily runs bring a shared date seed and preset gear to a more compact Draw your blade menu.
  • Temple completion uses a diagonal stripe; Armoury markers are thinner and equipped labels are removed.
  • Scattered Armour sends an enemy’s clothing and weapon flying. Seals and crests now use textured ink artwork.
  • Try rolling scroll menus in Options → Display → Menus. Classic remains available.
  • Share cards have been removed. The privacy policy now links to an AI disclosure.

1.40.3

  • All stages use softer, faded scenery colours for distant trees, rocks and landmarks, with a subtler effect on nearby objects.
  • Solid scenery now hides objects behind it, fixing mountains showing through the Demon skull.

1.40.2

  • Cinematic scenes switch immediately again, without the swipe transition.
  • Fully donated Temple upgrades move to the end; other upgrades keep their order.
  • The Demon realm gains tinted distant mountains and a softer, faded obelisk.

1.40.1

  • Maxed Temple upgrades use warm vermilion colours to match the ink-and-paper theme.
  • Demon scenery replaces the stone floor with short, sparse ash-coloured grass.

1.40.0

  • Temple upgrade colours show affordability and maxed ranks, with no ready counts or title-menu indicators.
  • Demon joins the cinematic scenes, with varied landmarks and a clearer fractured stone floor. Scene changes swipe across the screen.
  • The original Mystic Rock companion is back. The player breathes subtly while idle.

1.39.0

  • Temple items, pause menus and other cards now have distinct brushed ink panel frames that resize without stretching their corners.

1.38.0

  • Companions now animate from individual parts: moving heads, tails and paws, articulated crow wings, and floating Mystic Rock shards.
  • Reduced motion keeps each companion in a still pose.

1.37.0

  • Temple marks affordable donations and fully completed upgrades, including a ready count on the title menu.
  • Try equipment in a cozy private Armoury room. Selected tabs keep their size.

1.36.0

  • Demon Mirror brings 13 waves of four mirrored enemies to a demonic landscape. Swipe opposite their blade and arrow.
  • Complete Demon Mirror to unlock Inferno, a film of amber flames and drifting embers.

1.35.0

  • Enemies carry different blades with individual angles, and breathe subtly while waiting.
  • The Issen logo appears first while the remaining artwork loads.

1.34.1

  • Hover now highlights locked Armoury previews and works with a mouse or trackpad on devices that also use touch input.

1.34.0

  • Buttons and selectable cards now use ink frames that keep their corners crisp at every size, with brighter highlights for selection and focus.

1.33.0

  • Every Temple upgrade and Trial now has a calligraphic brush emblem inspired by the Issen logo.

1.32.0

  • The Mirror never feints. His arrow and blade always point opposite to the required cut, including every cut in a counter chain.

1.31.0

  • Scene visits now reveal larger, distinct ink trees, bamboo and stone landmarks.
  • Regular enemies gain three clothing shapes and four new head silhouettes while retaining puppet animation.

1.30.0

  • Scene visits now vary a few trees, bamboo and rocks while preserving each stage's composition.
  • Changing cinematic scenes also refreshes the standing enemy lineup. Resizing keeps the current scene arrangement.

1.29.2

  • Enemy artwork now reuses bounded recolor and fog caches, preventing repeated pixel work when appearances vary.
  • Added reproducible sprite stress benchmarks and cache memory checks.

1.29.1

  • The Crow companion now perches on the shoulder through lean and reaction poses.
  • Removed floating grass tufts attached to enemies; the animated field grass stays unchanged.

1.29.0

  • Throwing knives refill for every duel; Temple descriptions now explain the cadence.
  • Secrets survive restoring a saved encounter. Visit cinematic mode to unlock the floating Mystic Rock companion.
  • Awakening effects follow the shape and bends of each blade profile.

1.28.0

  • Ink is now the only artwork style; animated grass, weather, spears and awakening effects remain.
  • Artwork readiness is checked before the scene begins.

1.27.0

  • Added release notes from the title screen. The link highlights updates since your last visit until you open it.

1.26.1

  • Added ink artwork for the First Strike and Pilgrim’s Bead charms.

1.26.0

  • Artwork loads before the game starts, with progress and a retry option.

1.25.0

  • Charms now share modular ink artwork.

1.24.0

  • All player blades use modular ink blade, handle and guard artwork.

1.23.0

  • Foreground bamboo frames Bamboo Road on computers and tablets.

1.22.0

  • Ink companions and more varied enemy appearances.

1.21.1

  • Ink is now the default artwork style.

1.21.0

  • Modular ink outfits for the player.

1.20.0

  • Modular ink enemies join the scene.
